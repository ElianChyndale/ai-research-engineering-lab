# Claim Checklist

## Evidence

- [ ] Every number in a claim points to a specific artifact
- [ ] The artifact is committed or hash-verified
- [ ] The run is identified by experiment ID and seed

## Fixture vs Research Result

- [ ] Is this a fixture run (synthetic data, educational)?
- [ ] Or is this a reviewed research result (real data, human-validated)?
- [ ] The distinction is clearly stated in the claim

## Correlation vs Causation

- [ ] Am I claiming A causes B, or just that A correlates with B?
- [ ] Have I controlled for confounders?

## Synthetic vs Real Market

- [ ] Am I claiming results on synthetic data apply to real markets?
- [ ] If so, what validation supports this transfer?

## Model Sensitivity vs Observed Calibration

- [ ] Am I reporting sensitivity analysis or just one run?
- [ ] Is calibration measured on held-out data?

## Implemented vs Independently Verified

- [ ] Did I implement this myself, or reproduce a published result?
- [ ] Is the implementation independently verified?

## No Production/Regulatory Claims

- [ ] I am not claiming production readiness
- [ ] I am not claiming regulatory compliance
- [ ] I am not claiming superiority over established libraries
