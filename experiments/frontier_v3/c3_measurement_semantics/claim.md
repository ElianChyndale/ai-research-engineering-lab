# C3 Claim — Scientific Measurement-Semantic Failure

**One-line claim:** Frontier multimodal/scientific models may encode semantic
similarity better than typed measurement structure, so controlled perturbations
to measurement semantics (unit, axis, scale, legend, uncertainty, equation
binding) cause disproportionately large reasoning failures vs matched surface
perturbations.

**Why cheap to falsify:** synthetic scientific figures generated from known
equations, 8 transformation families with matched surface controls, a
deterministic typed-measurement constraint checker, and a model adapter. If
`Delta_mechanism < 15pp` aggregate or OCR dominates, C3 is killed.

**Non-goals:** no frontier-model training; no claim that a specific model is
"bad"; the mechanism is the question.
