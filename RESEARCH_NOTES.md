# Research Notes

The lab is intentionally small and deterministic. Its purpose is not to beat public benchmarks, but to show that each portfolio method can be reduced to a reproducible experiment with baselines, outputs, and limitations.

## Shared Pattern

1. Define a small decision or retrieval problem.
2. Generate or load deterministic local data.
3. Compare a simple baseline against a slightly stronger method.
4. Write results under `research/results/`.
5. Record limitations and failure modes.
